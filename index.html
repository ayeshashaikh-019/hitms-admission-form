Welcome to HITMS

Welcome to the HITMS Admission System. This website provides basic admission information and an online application form for students who want to apply to undergraduate programs.

Hyderabad Institute for Technology & Management Sciences (HITMS) is a public-sector degree-awarding institute located in Hyderabad, Sindh, Pakistan.

Undergraduate Programs

Students can explore the following technology-focused programs:

  • BS Computer Science
  • BS Software Engineering
  • BS FinTech
  • BS Cyber Security
  • BS AI & Robotics
  • BS Data Science
  • BS Information Technology

Admission Eligibility

According to the HITMS undergraduate admissions information, applicants should have an HSC Pre-Engineering, Computer Science or equivalent qualification and normally require at least 50% marks in Intermediate. An institutional entry test is also listed as part of the assessment process.

Pre-medical candidates may be eligible after completing additional Mathematics requirements.

Required Documents

  1. SSC / Matric Marksheet and Certificate
  2. HSC Part I & Part II Marksheets
  3. Candidate CNIC or B-Form
  4. Four recent passport-size photographs

Admission Process

  1. Read the admission requirements.
  2. Select your desired program.
  3. Fill out the online application form.
  4. Provide the required personal and academic information.
  5. Submit the application for processing.
